A quick quiz for anyone still nodding along in class. Each term gets a three-second countdown before the answer shows up.

The three terms

  1. Omoplata: a shoulder lock applied with your legs instead of your arms, usually from guard.
  2. Ezekiel choke: a choke using your own sleeve (or a bare forearm in no-gi) across the opponent's neck, often from mount.
  3. Kimura: a shoulder lock named after Masahiko Kimura, who used it to win a legendary judo match.
