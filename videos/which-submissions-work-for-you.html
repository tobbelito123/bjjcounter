Which submissions actually work for you?

Last updated October 18, 2026 · 0:32

Watch on YouTube

After a couple of months of logging, the list starts telling you something. This clip uses eight weeks of example data to show where to look.

What happens in the video

  1. Tap Sort, and the moves you hit most jump to the top.
  2. The small belt next to each move levels up as you land it on tougher partners.
  3. The Stats tab splits every technique by the belt of the person you got it on.
  4. The journey bar at the top moves one belt for every hundred logged taps.

In the example, the armbar is mostly a white belt finish while the triangle still lands on purples. That kind of gap is useful to know, because it tells you which move to drill against better partners. The belt bar is just a motivation tool, not a promotion predictor, and how belt promotions actually work explains why no app can tell you that.

The data in the video is an example. Yours fills up as you train.
