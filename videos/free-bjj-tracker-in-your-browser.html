BJJ Submissions Counter isn't in an app store. It's a web app, so it opens straight from the address bar and costs nothing.

What happens in the video

  1. Type bjjcounter.com in your phone's browser. It opens directly into the counter.
  2. Tap Add and type a technique that isn't in the default list. The video uses a loop choke.
  3. The new move shows up right away and logs like any other.

Put it on your home screen

On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app. After that it opens full screen and works offline, which helps in gyms with no signal.

If you're not sure what a move is called before you add it, the BJJ glossary has the common names.
