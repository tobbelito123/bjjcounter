The flame at the top of the counter shows how many days in a row you have logged at least one submission. This clip starts on day six and logs the first sub of day seven.

What happens in the video

  1. The counter opens with six days in a row already logged.
  2. A rear naked choke on a blue belt goes in, the first sub of the day.
  3. The streak jumps to seven and the app calls out the milestone.
  4. Skip a day and the count starts over.

The app celebrates at 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days and beyond. A streak is a nudge, not a training plan, though. Rest days matter, and how often beginners should actually train is the better guide to how many days a week makes sense.
