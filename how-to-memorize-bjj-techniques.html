How to Memorize BJJ Techniques Faster

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu involves hundreds of techniques, guards, and submissions. Remembering the exact steps for a complex sweep or a submission defense can be challenging without the right strategy.

Turn Training Logs into Flashcards

Writing down your training logs is a great start, but reviewing them passively won't make them stick. The secret is converting your notes into test questions.

If you want to optimize your study time off the mats, you can convert your BJJ training logs into interactive AI quizzes on SwipeSmrt. Upload a photo of your notebook or technique lists to generate custom flashcards in seconds.

Memorizing technique is half the battle. Track what's actually working

Flashcards help the steps stick. BJJ Submissions Counter tracks which of those techniques you're actually finishing live, and against which belt level, so you know which ones are worth keeping in the drilling rotation. It's free, there's no account to make, and it works offline.
